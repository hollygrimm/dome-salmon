Dome cross-section — looking upstream (west)

Two Axes

→

Horizontal — Time

Headwaters = 1986 (past, abundance). Estuary = present (sparse, arriving). Walking upstream is walking into memory.

↑

Vertical — Afterlife

Floor = river = living fish. Ceiling = forest = dissolved ancestors. Years of completed runs accumulate above as ghost canopy.

Color by lifecycle stage

  • ■ Ocean silver — entering from estuary
  • ■ River copper — mid-journey
  • ■ Spawning red — near headwaters
  • ■ Ghost — dissolved, rising

Abundance → density

Each year's spawner count (ODFW data, 1986–2026) drives boid population. 1992: ~18,000. 2010: ~32,000. 2019: 275. The viewer feels the difference as space.

Dentalium strand

A relational thread running the river's length — offered to the water, absorbed into the anatomy of the river. Present across all years.

The viewer's movement

Walking toward the headwaters is walking toward abundance, toward the ancestral density, toward a ceiling thick with what was. Walking back is walking toward now.

The dome geometry

Floor = river = present tense of each run. Ceiling = forest = accumulated past. The vertical axis is the afterlife axis. The horizontal axis is the time axis.

The argument without words

Near the estuary, 2018–2022: thin water, bare ceiling. The emptiness is not absence — it is the shape of what has not yet returned to give itself.
